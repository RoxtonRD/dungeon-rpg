# Lore Brief — fill this in, in your own voice

> You write the world. I turn it into `lore-bible.md` and then into the
> ~40–60 translation keys that put it on screen.
>

> the build**. Nothing here asks you to invent a system. If an answer
> would require new mechanics, it's the wrong answer — tell me and we'll
> talk about it separately.

## How to answer

- **Fragments, not essays.** Names, one-liners, images. Two or three
  sentences per question is plenty. The game delivers lore in a 2-line
  room label and a 3-line event box — write at that size and it will
  survive contact with the screen.
- **Write in pt-BR.** It's the source language; English is the
  translation. Proper nouns especially — name things in Portuguese first
  and let the English follow. (Mix languages freely if it's faster; I'll
  sort it.)
- **"Pass" is a real answer.** A blank I can flag beats filler I have to
  work around. Skip anything that doesn't spark.
- **Contradict yourself freely.** Reconciling is my job.

## The six names I need

Everything else can stay unnamed for now. These six carry the whole
world, and every one of them already has a hole in the build waiting for
it:

1. The place below Praesidium — the one with a bottom
> Praesidium is the world name. We're at the a medium sized town of Raveraos. Nearby the town, there's the mysterious place know as "Cursed Crypt".
1b. The place that opens once you've reached that bottom — the one without
> After defeating the last boss, the party find out the boss just broke an ancient seal, opening the "Abyssal Fissure", just at the side of the Crypt. Abyssal monsters start to leave the fissure, heroes should go against the unknow darkness and fight back. 
2. What the Priestess serves
> She serves "Dhaliria", the moon goddess, of healing, love and passion.
3. What the Dark Altar serves
> It's unkonow for the heroes, but the it serves "Algobaz", an powerful devil entity.
4. The Wandering Merchant
> His identity may remain unknow, but he's a free will undead, scavenger and merchant, named "Samiro".
5. Whatever brings the party back after a wipe
> Really we can work some sort of random events, like "Another Party", "The Wandering Merchant", "A Mysterious Light", "The Hospitaler Brigade", and might add some more, each one with a percentage chance, and effects.
6. The thing at the very bottom
> "Faesto", an poweful abyssal mage, obsessed with power, wanted to find a direct source of abyssal power, breaking an ancient magic seal that closed an abyssal fissure nearby. The party just realize that after arriving. He's alongside with "Halios", an undead ancient necromancer, that was corrupted by his abyssal magic, to turn against it's mission, that was to protect the seal.

## §1 — The Descent

*Anchor: four floors, a boss, then home. Then you go back down, and it's
harder. Repeat. Player-facing, this is "Enter Dungeon Lv 1, 2, 3…".*

**1.1 — What is Praesidium, and what is underneath it?** One sentence
each. The name is currently the only fiction in the entire game.

> Praesidium é o nome do mundo, a cidade que estamos é uma cidade média, chamada Raveraos, próximo à cidade, está a Cripta Amaldiçoada

**1.2 — Why does the party go down? And why do they go back down, deeper,
after already surviving it once?** The mundane, personal or economic
reason is worth more here than a cosmic one — it's the loop's motivation
and the player repeats it every session.

> São aventureiros em busca de fama, fortuna, conhecimento e poder. Mortos-vivos começaram a sair da Cripta, o que não acontecia, então mercadores e cidadãos preocupados ofereceram recompensas por caçadas e respostas.

**1.3 — There are two places now, and they are different in kind.**
(Decided in `roadmap-v3.md` Move 3: a campaign dungeon you can finish,
which then unlocks an endless one.) So:

- The first has a **bottom**, and you reach it. What's down there, and
  what does reaching it change?
- The second has **none**. Why not? Is it older, bigger, not a place at
  all, still being made?
- And the question that ties them: **why does finishing the first one
  open the second?** Did you unseal it, earn it, or attract its
  attention?

> _(sua resposta)_

*What this decides:* the two names in the list above, what the city
button says instead of "Dungeon Lv 2", and the ending beat of the
campaign. This is the highest-value question in the brief right now.
